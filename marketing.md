## Steam Page

- This checklist must be complete before setting up a Steam Page
  1. You know your exact genre
  2. Your art style is locked
  3. You can show 3 unique environments
  4. You have a professional capsule image
  5. You have a gameplay trailer
- Allocate 1 month for actually setting up the Steam page itself
- Create a developer home page
- Have translations for your Steam Page
