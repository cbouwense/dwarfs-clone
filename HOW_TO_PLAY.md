# The Goal

Don't lose!

# How to lose

You lose when your town hall is destroyed. This can happen by water touching it, lava touching it, or goblins destroying it.

# Core Mechanics

Managing the Miners: Your Miner Dwarfs (the green ones) spawn automatically from the Town Hall and immediately start chewing through the dirt in random directions. You do not have direct control over them.

Earning Gold: Dwarfs mine standard dirt for a slow trickle of gold, but striking mineral veins and treasure rooms brings in massive score boosts.

Guiding Dwarfs: You can spend your hard-earned gold to place Direction Arrows on the floor. When a dwarf walks over an arrow, they will turn and mine in that specific direction. You have to use these arrows to steer them toward riches or redirect them away from obvious danger.

Dealing with Disasters:
As your dwarfs dig outward, they will uncover dark, unexplored caves. These caves can contain treasure, but they just as frequently contain deadly hazards. Because every defensive action you take costs gold (your score), playing well requires balancing safety with profit.

1. Water and Lava
If a dwarf breaches a cave filled with water or lava, the liquid will flood your tunnel network. Water will quickly drown your dwarfs and destroy the Town Hall, while lava moves slower but burns through walls. To actually 
Walls: You can pay to build temporary walls or permanently "solidify" intact dirt to hold back the flow.

Dynamite: The most effective way to stop a catastrophic flood is to pay a dwarf to trigger dynamite at a tunnel choke point. This blows a bottomless pit into the floor, allowing the advancing water or lava to harmlessly drain away.

2. Monsters
Uncovering a monster den unleashes enemies like goblins, spiders, and bosses that will actively hunt down your diggers.

Warrior Dwarfs: You can spend gold to spawn heavily armed Warrior Dwarfs from your Town Hall, or you can build Outposts deeper in the map to spawn them closer to the action.

The Dwarf Cannon: Warriors only patrol their immediate local area and move slowly. To get them where they are needed quickly, you can literally load them into an Outpost's roof cannon and launch them across the map directly into the fray.

Key insight: The entire game is an economic balancing act. You need gold to buy arrows, walls, dynamite, and warriors — but the only way to get gold is to let your dwarfs dig, which inevitably causes the disasters you need to pay to fix.
