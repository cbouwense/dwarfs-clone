# Selection clearing

- given outpost menu is open
    - when right click is pressed
        - then the outpost menu is closed, but no other right click actions occur
    - when left click is pressed
        - then the outpost menu is closed, but no other left click actions occur
    - when a control mode becomes active
        - then the outpost menu is closed

- given outpost menu is closed
    - when right click is down
        - then the camera moves along with mouse movement

- given a control mode is open
    - when right click is pressed
        - then the control mode goes away

- given an outpost cannon perimeter is being shown
    - when right click is down
        - then the outpost cannon perimeter stays and the camera moves along with mouse movement
    - when right click is pressed and the camera has moved
        - then the outpost cannon perimeter stays
    - when right click is pressed and the camera has not moved
        - then the outpost cannon perimeter goes away

# Actions

- Close outpost menu if
    1. an outpost menu is open
    2. right or left click are pressed, or a control mode is activated
 
- Close control mode
- Close outpost cannon perimeter
- Drag camera
